## What it does

<!-- Stable description of the program. Carried forward from the previous note; edit only when the product itself changes.
     3 to 6 lines: what it is, who uses it, where it runs (URL/port/binary), how to start it. -->
POSIX processes, signals and unix sockets for Mojo: spawn a child with its own working directory, environment, process group and log file; reap it without blocking; receive signals as a pollable file descriptor; wait on many descriptors with `poll`; serve and connect over unix sockets. The standard library's process API cannot set cwd, env or a process group.

## Highlights

<!-- 1 to 5 bullets a user would notice. Lead with the benefit, not the implementation. -->
- Drive a child over a pipe-like socket: `spawn_pair` starts a program with its stdin and stdout joined to one end of a unix socketpair and hands back the other end, ready for `recv`, `send_all` and `PollSet`. Writing to a child that died returns `False` instead of killing your process with SIGPIPE.

## Changes

### Added
- `spawn_pair(argv, env, cwd) -> (pid, fd)`: our end non-blocking and close-on-exec, the child's end blocking, stderr inherited.

### Changed
- `try_spawn` and `spawn_pair` share one `posix_spawnp` core; behaviour of `spawn` and `try_spawn` is unchanged.
